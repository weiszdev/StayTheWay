Page 2 · A Note for Grown-Ups

👋 A Note for Grown-Ups

This little booklet walks through the 28 spiritual gifts found in Scripture — one gift per page, in words a 5–10 year old can grab onto.

How to use it:

  • Go slow. One gift a night (or a week) is plenty. It's a treasure hunt, not a test.
  • Read together. Each page has what the gift is, a Bible story, an everyday kid example, and a "Talk together" question.
  • Watch for sparkle. When your child lights up — "That's me!" — circle that page number on the back cover.
  • Pages marked ✦ touch on gifts Christians understand in different ways, or that need a gentle grown-up touch. You know your child best — guide the conversation your way.

Every gift comes from the same giver, and none is for keeping — every one is for sharing. There are no wrong answers here.

Page 3 · What Is a Spiritual Gift?

🎈 Hey Kids! What's a Spiritual Gift?

When you love Jesus, God's Holy Spirit gives you a special ability — a gift! Not the kind wrapped in paper. A gift that lives inside you.

Here's the secret about God's gifts:

  • 🎁 They come from God — you don't have to earn them.
  • 🤝 They're for helping other people, not just for you.
  • 🧩 Everybody gets different ones — that's why we need each other, like puzzle pieces!

In this booklet you'll meet 28 gifts. As you read, ask yourself:

"Does this one feel like ME?" 🌟

The Bible says: "There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit gives them all." — 1 Corinthians 12:4

Page 4 · Gift 1
📣Gift 1 of 28

Prophecy

The Truth-Teller Gift
Romans 12:6
What it is

God helps some people hear what He wants to say — and say it out loud, bravely, even when it's hard.

📖 In the Bible

Jonah told the people of Nineveh God's message, and the whole city turned back to God! (Jonah 3)

🏠 It might look like…

Kindly telling your friends, "That's not fair, and God wants us to be fair" — even when it's not the popular thing to say.

💬 Talk together

Is it ever hard to say what's true? When?
