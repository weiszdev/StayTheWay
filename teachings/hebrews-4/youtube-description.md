## Description

HEBREWS 4 — The Rest, The Word, The High Priest

Hebrews Chapter 4 is one of the clearest reminders in Scripture that
God's Word is not merely information — it is alive, penetrating, and
able to reveal what is happening deep within us.

In this study, we walk through Hebrews 4 and look at the call to enter
God's rest, the danger of unbelief, the power of the Word of God, and
the incredible truth that Jesus is our great High Priest.

Four beats: a promise still standing, a warning still sounding, a tool
still cutting, and a throne still open.

KEY VERSE
"For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any
twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and
spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the
thoughts and intents of the heart." — Hebrews 4:12 KJV
