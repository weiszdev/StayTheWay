This Week’s Teaching

Hebrews 4 is one of the clearest reminders in Scripture that God’s Word is not merely information — it is alive, penetrating, and able to reveal what is happening deep within us. The chapter moves in four beats: a promise still standing (rest), a warning still sounding (unbelief), a tool still cutting (the Word), and a throne still open (our great High Priest).

The Four Movements
  1. 1. The Rest That Remains — Hebrews 4:1–11 The promise did not expire in the wilderness. It is still open — and entering means ceasing from your own works.
  2. 2. The Danger of Unbelief — Hebrews 4:2, 6, 11 They heard the same gospel we did. It did not profit them, because it was not mixed with faith.
  3. 3. The Word That Cuts — Hebrews 4:12–13 Living, powerful, sharper than any two-edged sword — and a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.
  4. 4. Our Great High Priest — Hebrews 4:14–16 Tempted in every point as we are, yet without sin. So come boldly to the throne of grace.
This Week’s Challenge

Open the Word this week and let it examine you before you use it to examine anyone else. Then bring what it exposes to the throne of grace — boldly, and in time of need.
