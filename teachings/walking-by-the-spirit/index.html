8-Week Teaching Series

Walking By the Spirit

From the Shepherd's voice to the Spirit's visible fruit. An eight-week guided arc through the gospel mechanism that moves a believer from hearing to following to bearing fruit.

"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Against such there is no law." — Galatians 5:22–23 (NKJV)

The Arc

🗪

Hear

"My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me." — John 10:27

👣

Follow

Hearing produces following. Identity reshapes role. The Pillars stand because the Shepherd spoke.

🍇

Bear Fruit

The fruit is not the program. The fruit is the proof. Walk by the Spirit and the fruit will come.
