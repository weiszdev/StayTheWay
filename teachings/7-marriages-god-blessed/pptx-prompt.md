## Prompt

Create a **vertical 9:16 PowerPoint presentation** (7.5" wide × 13.333" tall) for a church teaching titled **"7 Marriages God Blessed — Grace Over Judgment"**.

### Layout

- **Camera band:** Y 4.45"–8.85", full width, 4.4" tall — leave this zone EMPTY with a subtle dashed muted outline. The speaker's camera feed overlays here during live stream.
- Content goes ABOVE and BELOW the camera band only.
- **20 slides total.**

### Design

- **Background:** Deep navy (#000014)
- **Accent colors:** Gold (#D4A847), warm rose (#C97B7B), blue (#1478F0), sky (#28A0F0), white (#FFFFFF)
- **Fonts:** Georgia (headings), Helvetica Neue or Calibri (body)
- **Slide numbers** in bottom-right corner
- **StayTheWay Ministry** watermark or footer on each slide
- Visual motif: subtle interlocking rings or vine/branch pattern — marriage + "abide in me"

### Series Arc Context

This is Week 10 of an extended arc. The opening should briefly acknowledge:
- Weeks 1–2: Faithful Man / Faithful Woman (individual identity)
- Weeks 3–4: Psalm 40 / Fake Christians (worship + self-examination)
- Weeks 5–7: Romans 7–8 / Galatians 5 (war within, life in Spirit, fruit vs. flesh)
- Week 8: Pulling It Together — The Servant
- Week 9: Jonah — God's response to disobedience
- **Now Week 10: Two people walking together. Marriage.**

---

**Slide 3 — Marriage #1, Slide A: ABRAHAM & SARAH**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "#1 — ABRAHAM & SARAH"
  - Tag: "The Promise Keepers"
  - **Blessing:** God built an entire nation through their marriage — even when they couldn't see how.
  - **Complement:** Abraham was the risk-taker — leaving everything on God's word. Sarah was the builder — creating a home in the wilderness, managing the household, enduring decades of waiting.
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Shortcoming:** Sarah gave Hagar to Abraham (Gen 16). Abraham went along with it. Both tried to manufacture God's promise through human engineering.
  - **Grace Moment:** Abraham never threw it back in her face. Sarah never blamed him for agreeing. When Isaac came, they celebrated together — not "I told you so" but "God kept His word."

**Slide 4 — Marriage #1, Slide B: ABRAHAM & SARAH (Scripture)**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "By faith Sarah herself also received strength to conceive seed, and she bore a child when she was past the age, because she judged Him faithful who had promised." — Hebrews 11:11 (NKJV)
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Jesus Bridge:** Jesus never reminds us of the shortcuts we took before we trusted Him. He fulfills the promise — not our attempts to help Him.
  - **Applied:** Where are you and your spouse trying to manufacture what God has promised? Name it. Let it go. Wait together.
  - See also: Genesis 12:1-4 · Genesis 16 · Genesis 21:1-7 · 1 Peter 3:6

---

**Slide 5 — Marriage #2, Slide A: ISAAC & REBEKAH**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "#2 — ISAAC & REBEKAH"
  - Tag: "The Prayed-For Pair"
  - **Blessing:** Isaac prayed for his barren wife for 20 years — and God answered with twin nations.
  - **Complement:** Isaac was steady, quiet, meditative (Gen 24:63). Rebekah was decisive, bold, hospitable — she watered ten camels without being asked. He brought the patience; she brought the courage.
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Shortcoming:** Rebekah deceived Isaac to secure the blessing for Jacob (Gen 27). She played favorites. He was passive.
  - **Grace Moment:** Isaac, even after discovering the deception, confirmed the blessing instead of cursing. He chose God's sovereignty over his own hurt.

**Slide 6 — Marriage #2, Slide B: ISAAC & REBEKAH (Scripture)**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "Now Isaac pleaded with the LORD for his wife, because she was barren; and the LORD granted his plea, and Rebekah his wife conceived." — Genesis 25:21 (NKJV)
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Jesus Bridge:** Jesus, even when we manipulate circumstances instead of trusting His timing, still confirms the blessing. He doesn't withdraw grace because we mishandled the process.
  - **Applied:** Are you praying for your spouse — or just frustrated with them? Shift from complaint to intercession this week.
  - See also: Genesis 24:63-67 · Genesis 27 · Romans 9:10-13

---

**Slide 7 — Marriage #3, Slide A: BOAZ & RUTH**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "#3 — BOAZ & RUTH"
  - Tag: "The Redeemer & The Redeemed"
  - **Blessing:** A Moabite widow and a Bethlehem landowner produced the line of King David — and of Jesus.
  - **Complement:** Ruth brought loyalty beyond reason — she left everything to follow. Boaz brought protection, provision, and dignity — he didn't just help, he honored her publicly.
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Shortcoming:** Ruth's bold move on the threshing floor (Ruth 3) was risky and unconventional — it could have been misread or exploited.
  - **Grace Moment:** Boaz didn't shame her for coming to the threshing floor. He called her "blessed" and protected her reputation. He covered her — literally and figuratively.

**Slide 8 — Marriage #3, Slide B: BOAZ & RUTH (Scripture)**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "And now, my daughter, do not fear. I will do for you all that you request, for all the people of my town know that you are a virtuous woman." — Ruth 3:11 (NKJV)
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Jesus Bridge:** Jesus is our Kinsman-Redeemer. He doesn't exploit our vulnerability — He covers it. He doesn't shame our past — He calls us "blessed" and redeems our story.
  - **Applied:** How can you cover your spouse this week — protect their reputation, honor their vulnerability, speak well of them publicly?
  - See also: Ruth 1:16-17 · Ruth 2:8-12 · Ruth 4:13-17 · Matthew 1:5

---

**Slide 9 — Marriage #4, Slide A: ELKANAH & HANNAH**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "#4 — ELKANAH & HANNAH"
  - Tag: "The Comforter & The Surrenderer"
  - **Blessing:** Hannah's surrendered son Samuel became Israel's greatest prophet-judge, anointing both Saul and David.
  - **Complement:** Hannah brought radical devotion — pouring out her soul before God with tears nobody else understood. Elkanah brought faithful presence — he loved her extra, stayed close, and didn't pretend her pain was small.
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Shortcoming:** Elkanah's clumsy question: "Am I not better to you than ten sons?" (1 Sam 1:8). He meant well but minimized her grief.
  - **Grace Moment:** Hannah didn't lash out at his poor words. She took her pain to God instead — and Elkanah let her go to Shiloh, supported her vow, and never tried to take it back.

**Slide 10 — Marriage #4, Slide B: ELKANAH & HANNAH (Scripture)**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "For this child I prayed, and the LORD has granted me my petition which I asked of Him. Therefore I also have lent him to the LORD; as long as he lives he shall be lent to the LORD." — 1 Samuel 1:27-28 (NKJV)
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Jesus Bridge:** Jesus doesn't say "Am I not enough?" when we grieve. He weeps with us (John 11:35). He sits in our pain before He speaks to it.
  - **Applied:** When your spouse is hurting, are you fixing or sitting? This week, choose presence over solutions.
  - See also: 1 Samuel 2:1-10 · 1 Samuel 2:18-21

---

**Slide 11 — Marriage #5, Slide A: AQUILA & PRISCILLA**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "#5 — AQUILA & PRISCILLA"
  - Tag: "The Co-Laborers"
  - **Blessing:** They hosted house churches across the Roman Empire, discipled Apollos, and risked their necks for Paul.
  - **Complement:** Always named together — never one without the other. Priscilla is named first in 4 of 6 references, suggesting she may have been the primary teacher. Aquila brought the trade (tentmaking); together they built a platform for the gospel.
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Shortcoming:** They were refugees — expelled from Rome under Claudius (Acts 18:2). Displaced, uprooted, starting over with nothing.
  - **Grace Moment:** Neither is recorded blaming the other for their circumstances. They turned exile into mission — every city they landed in became a church plant.

**Slide 12 — Marriage #5, Slide B: AQUILA & PRISCILLA (Scripture)**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "Greet Priscilla and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus, who risked their own necks for my life, to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles. Likewise greet the church that is in their house." — Romans 16:3-5 (NKJV)
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Jesus Bridge:** Jesus turned His own displacement (heaven to earth) into the greatest rescue mission in history. He turns our disruptions into deployments.
  - **Applied:** Are you and your spouse building something together for God — or just coexisting? Name one thing you can serve in as a team.
  - See also: Acts 18:1-4 · Acts 18:24-26 · 1 Corinthians 16:19 · 2 Timothy 4:19

---

**Slide 13 — Marriage #6, Slide A: JOSEPH & MARY**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "#6 — JOSEPH & MARY"
  - Tag: "The Trusters"
  - **Blessing:** They raised the Son of God. The most consequential parenting assignment in human history.
  - **Complement:** Mary brought radical surrender — "Let it be to me according to your word." Joseph brought quiet obedience — he didn't need the spotlight, just the next instruction. She said yes publicly; he obeyed privately.
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Shortcoming:** Joseph's first instinct was to divorce her quietly (Matt 1:19). He assumed the worst before God spoke.
  - **Grace Moment:** When the angel corrected him, Joseph didn't defend his assumption — he immediately obeyed. And Mary never held his initial doubt against him. No record of resentment.

**Slide 14 — Marriage #6, Slide B: JOSEPH & MARY (Scripture)**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "Then Joseph, being aroused from sleep, did as the angel of the Lord commanded him and took to him his wife." — Matthew 1:24 (NKJV)
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Jesus Bridge:** Jesus doesn't hold our first reaction against us. He gives us the dream, the word, the correction — and waits for obedience, not perfection.
  - **Applied:** Where has God corrected your first instinct about your spouse? Have you obeyed — or are you still holding the original assumption?
  - See also: Luke 1:26-38 · Luke 2:41-52 · Matthew 2:13-23

---

**Slide 15 — Marriage #7, Slide A: CHRIST & THE CHURCH**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "#7 — CHRIST & THE CHURCH"
  - Tag: "The Ultimate Marriage"
  - **Blessing:** Every earthly marriage is a shadow of this one. He gave everything; we are made spotless.
  - **Complement:** Christ brings sacrificial initiative — He loved first, died first, serves first. The Church brings responsive devotion — we love because He first loved us. He initiates; we respond. He gives; we receive and reflect.
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Shortcoming:** The Church — the bride — is unfaithful. Over and over. Idolatry, lukewarmness, division, pride. Every sin in the book.
  - **Grace Moment:** He doesn't divorce His bride. He doesn't give up. He intercedes, sanctifies, corrects, and keeps presenting us "without spot or wrinkle." Grace upon grace upon grace.

**Slide 16 — Marriage #7, Slide B: CHRIST & THE CHURCH (Scripture)**
- ABOVE CAMERA:
  - "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself for her, that He might sanctify and cleanse her with the washing of water by the word, that He might present her to Himself a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but that she should be holy and without blemish." — Ephesians 5:25-27 (NKJV)
- BELOW CAMERA:
  - **Jesus Bridge:** This IS the bridge. Every marriage before this one was a rehearsal. Every act of grace one spouse gives another is an echo of what Christ does for us daily.
  - **Applied:** Which of the 6 marriages above most mirrors your own? Where has your spouse shown you grace you didn't deserve? Tell them — out loud — before you go to bed tonight.
  - See also: Revelation 19:7-9 · Revelation 21:2 · Hosea 2:19-20 · 2 Corinthians 11:2
