Series Arc

Over 9 weeks we examined the faithful man, the faithful woman, Psalm 40’s new song, fake Christians, the war within (Romans 7), life in the Spirit (Romans 8), fruit vs. flesh, the servant heart, and God’s response to disobedience in Jonah. Now we look at two people walking together — marriage, the first institution God created and the metaphor He chose for Christ and the Church.

The 7 Marriages
  1. 1. Abraham & Sarah — The Promise Keepers
  2. 2. Isaac & Rebekah — The Prayed-For Pair
  3. 3. Boaz & Ruth — The Redeemer & The Redeemed
  4. 4. Elkanah & Hannah — The Comforter & The Surrenderer
  5. 5. Aquila & Priscilla — The Co-Laborers
  6. 6. Joseph & Mary — The Trusters
  7. 7. Christ & The Church — The Ultimate Marriage
