## SERIES ARC REVIEW (for opening remarks)

Use this to set up the teaching — remind people where we've been:

> Over the last 9 weeks, we've been walking through what it means to walk by the Spirit.
>
> We started with the **man** — 7 pillars of faithfulness. Then the **woman** — identity before performance.
> We sang the **New Song** in Psalm 40. We looked in the mirror at **7 IDs of a fake Christian**.
> We named the **war within** in Romans 7 — and found **life in the Spirit** in Romans 8.
> We examined the **Fruit and the Flesh** in Galatians 5. We **pulled it all together** as servants.
> Last week we studied **Jonah** — God's response when we run.
>
> But we've been looking at individuals. Today we look at **two people walking together**.
> Marriage is the first institution God created — before government, before church, before nation.
> And it's the metaphor God chose for Christ and the Church.
>
> **7 marriages. 7 blessings. 7 moments where one spouse gave grace instead of judgment.
> And every single one of them points to Jesus — the Bridegroom who gave Himself for His bride.**

## TEACHING STRUCTURE

For each of the 7 marriages:

1. **The Couple** — who they are, their context
2. **The Blessing** — what God did through their marriage
3. **How They Complement** — what each spouse brought that the other didn't
4. **The Shortcoming** — where one (or both) failed
5. **The Grace Moment** — how the spouse responded with grace over judgment
6. **The Jesus Bridge** — how this mirrors what Christ does for the Church
7. **Applied Step** — one actionable thing for couples this week
