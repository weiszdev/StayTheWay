Psalm 65:12 — even the dry hills sing for joy. So can you.

For Parents & Teachers

Today's big idea: staying close to God — every day, not just Sunday — is what keeps joy flowing, even in hard "wilderness" seasons. Anchor verse: Psalm 65:11–13, with Proverbs 13:1 on staying near enough to hear the Father's instruction.

Walk the trail together: read the Story stops out loud, say the Verse rounds together (little ones can repeat after you), then play the quiz as a family. The hills bloom as your child moves through the trail — a picture of Psalm 65:12.
