“Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he shall hear my voice.” — Psalm 55:17 (KJV)
Never really met the Father?

This whole teaching is about hearing the Father again. But you can't come back to a Father you've never known, and the good news is that He made the way.

“But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.” — Romans 5:8

“Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.” — John 14:6

“That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.” — Romans 10:9

His goodness doesn't wait for you to reach fertile ground. It drops on the wilderness you're already standing in. If you're ready, pray this from your heart:

Father, I've lost my way, and I've been trying to live on bread alone. I confess that I'm a sinner and I can't fix myself. I believe Jesus died for my sins and that You raised Him from the dead. Jesus, I confess You as my Lord. Come into my life, renew my mind, and teach me to hear Your voice every day. Thank You for Your goodness that reaches even my wilderness. In Jesus' name, Amen.
