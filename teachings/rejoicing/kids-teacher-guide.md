# Kids Teacher Guide — Rejoicing on Every Side
**StayTheWay Kids · Sunday, September 13, 2026 · Ages 5–10 · ~20 minutes**

## The one big idea
> **When we listen to God every day, His goodness fills up even our dry, grumpy places — and we can be happy on every side.**

## Key verses (simple words)
| Verse | Say it like this |
|---|---|
| **Proverbs 13:1** | A wise kid listens to his father. God is our Father — let's listen to Him! |
| **Psalm 55:17** | Morning, noon, and night, I talk to God, and He hears me. |
| **Matthew 4:4** | Food fills your tummy. God's Word fills your heart. |
| **Psalm 65:12** | God's goodness drops on the dry wilderness, and the little hills rejoice on every side! |

## Talking points (3 steps)
**1. Hear the Father** 👂
- Ask: *"What happens to a phone if you forget to charge it?"* (It dies! It can't do its job.)
- Our hearts are like that. We charge up by listening to God — reading the Bible and praying.
- Not just on Sunday! Morning (when you wake up), noon (at lunch), night (at bedtime).

**2. Name the drain** 🔋
- Ask: *"Have you ever felt grumpy and didn't know why?"*
- When we forget to listen to God, little complaints get louder and happy feelings get quieter.
- God made us with feelings — happy, sad, mad, scared. That's good! His Word helps our feelings not get all mixed up.

**3. Let it drop** ⛰️
- Draw a dry desert on the board. Then draw raindrops falling on it.
- Psalm 65 says God's goodness drops on the **wilderness** — the dry places — not just the pretty gardens.
- Then the hills get so happy they "rejoice on every side," the sheep fill the fields, the corn grows, and everything sings!

| 2 | We can only talk to God on Sunday. | **FALSE** — morning, noon, and night (Psalm 55:17) |

| 6 | In Psalm 65, the little hills rejoice on every side! | **TRUE** — Psalm 65:12 |
| 7 | God's goodness only goes to places that are already pretty. | **FALSE** — it drops on the wilderness |

## Discussion questions
1. When is a good time for YOU to talk to God in the morning? At noon? At night?
2. What makes your "battery" feel low? What helps charge it back up?
3. What's a "dry place" in your life — something that makes you sad or grumpy?
4. How do you think the little hills "rejoice"? Show me with your arms!
5. Who is someone you could tell about God's goodness this week?

## Closing prayer (kids repeat after you)
> Dear God, thank You for being my Father. Help me listen to You in the morning, at noon, and at night. When I feel grumpy or sad, fill me up with Your goodness. Make my heart happy on every side. In Jesus' name, Amen.
