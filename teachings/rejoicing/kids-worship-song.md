# Kids Worship Song — "The Little Hills Rejoice"

**Theme:** Listen to God every day, and His goodness makes even the dry places sing (Psalm 65:11–13)
**Ages:** 5–7 | **Key:** G Major | **Tempo:** 104 BPM (bouncy) | **Time:** 4/4
**Length:** ~2:30 | Hand motions in *[brackets]*

---

## Verse 1
```
G                 C          G
In the morning, I will pray  [arms rise up like the sun]
G                 D
At the noontime, too          [hands make a big circle overhead]
G                 C          G
In the night-time, I will say  [hands together, pillow on cheek]
    C       D        G
"Father God, I'm listening to You!"  [cup hand behind ear]
```

## Chorus
```
G               C
The little hills rejoice!     [arms make hill shapes, bounce them]
D               G
The little hills rejoice!
G                  C
On every side, on every side  [point left, then right]
D               G
The little hills rejoice!     [jump and wave both hands]
```

## Verse 2
```
G                  C         G
My phone needs charging every day  [plug two fingers together]
G                  D
My tummy needs its food        [rub tummy]
G                   C        G
But my heart needs God's Word to stay  [hands on heart]
    C         D          G
Filled up with all that's good!  [arms wide open]
```

## Bridge (Psalm 65:13)
```
C                  G
The sheep fill up the fields  [hands on head like sheep ears]
C                  G
The corn grows up so tall     [crouch down, stretch up tall]
C                  G
The valleys shout for joy     [hands around mouth]
    D
And sing, sing, sing it all!  [spin around]
```

## Final Chorus (twice, second time slower then big finish)
```
G               C
The little hills rejoice!
D               G
The little hills rejoice!
G                  C
On every side, on every side
D               G
The little hills rejoice!
```

---

## Song Notes
- **Chords:** only G, C, D — any beginner guitarist or keys player can lead it.
- **Energy:** verses bounce, chorus is the big movement moment, bridge builds with the "sing, sing, sing" spin.
- **Teaching tie-in:** Verse 1 = Step 1 (hear the Father morning, noon, night). Verse 2 = the phone/food pictures. Chorus + bridge = Step 3 (Psalm 65:12–13).
- **Echo option for pre-readers:** leader sings each line, kids echo it back.

## Scripture Anchors
- Proverbs 13:1 — "A wise son heareth his father's instruction"
- Psalm 55:17 — "Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray"
- Matthew 4:4 — "Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God"
- Psalm 65:12 — "The little hills rejoice on every side"
- Psalm 65:13 — "The pastures are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, they also sing"
