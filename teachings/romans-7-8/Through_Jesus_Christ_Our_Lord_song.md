# Through Jesus Christ Our Lord
*A worship song from Romans 7 · StayTheWay Ministry · key of A · ~74 BPM*

> Worship-focused, not "I"-focused. Every line is pointed at God's character and His work in Christ. Built around Romans 7:4 (Bridegroom / bear fruit to God) and Romans 7:25 ("I thank God — through Jesus Christ our Lord").

---

## Lyrics

**VERSE 1**
The law is holy, righteous, true —
A perfect picture, Lord, of You.
It showed us death, it showed us shame,
But could not save and could not change.

**VERSE 2**
So You sent the Son the law could not,
You bore the curse that sin had wrought.
The body broken, blood outpoured —
And we are free through Christ our Lord.

**PRE-CHORUS**
Lifted on a Roman tree,
Risen from the grave —
The Bridegroom of the wretched ones,
The Lord who came to save.

**CHORUS**
Glory to the One who raised Him from the grave!
Glory to the Father, glory to the Son who saves!
Through Jesus Christ our Lord —
Through Jesus Christ our Lord —
Be the worship, be the wonder, be the praise of all the earth,
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

**VERSE 3**
We were the bride of bondage's chain,
Married to death and bound to pain.
He came and won us with His blood,
He drew us up out of the flood.

**VERSE 4**
Now we bear fruit to God alone,
Now we belong, no more our own.
The grave could not hold down the King —
Forever He — the One we sing.

**PRE-CHORUS** *(repeat)*

**CHORUS** *(repeat)*

**BRIDGE** *(builds, four repeats, key lift on the third pass)*
Worthy, worthy, worthy is the Lamb.
Worthy, worthy, worthy I AM.
Holy, holy, holy is His Name —
Yesterday, today, forever the same.

**FINAL CHORUS** *(full band, congregation)*
Glory to the One who raised Him from the grave!
Glory to the Father, glory to the Son who saves!
Through Jesus Christ our Lord —
Through Jesus Christ our Lord —
Be the worship, be the wonder, be the praise of all the earth,
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

**OUTRO** *(soft, congregational)*
Holy, holy, holy —
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Holy, holy, holy —
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

## Chord Sketch (Key of A)

- **Verses:** A · F#m · D · E
- **Pre-Chorus:** F#m · D · A · E
- **Chorus:** A · E · F#m · D (with E/G# pickup into the second line)
- **Bridge:** D · A · F#m · E (lift the third pass to B major for emotional crest)

Tempo 72–76 BPM. Begin sparse (acoustic guitar + piano pad). Verse 2 add bass + light kick. Pre-Chorus pull back, then the Chorus is full band. Bridge climbs four passes — the third lifts a whole step into B major, the fourth lands back in A for the Final Chorus.

## Connection to Romans 7

Verse 1 → **Romans 7:12** ("the law is holy, and the commandment holy and just and good")
Verse 2 → **Romans 7:4 / 8:3** (what the law could not do, God did through His Son)
Pre-Chorus → **Romans 7:4** (the Bridegroom — "Him who was raised from the dead")
Verse 3 → **Romans 7:1-3, 4** (we were bound, He set us free)
Verse 4 → **Romans 7:4** ("that we should bear fruit to God")
Chorus / Outro → **Romans 7:25** ("I thank God — through Jesus Christ our Lord!")

No verse turns inward. Paul's *cry* in 7:24 is the silence the song fills; the song is the *answer* of 7:25 sung back to God.
