# If God Is For Us
*A worship song from Romans 8 · StayTheWay Ministry · key of D · ~78 BPM*

> Worship-focused, not "I"-focused. Every line is pointed at God's character and His work in the Father, Son, and Spirit. Built around Romans 8:1, 8:15, 8:31, and 8:38-39. Pairs with "Through Jesus Christ Our Lord" (Romans 7) — together they move from cry → answer, struggle → freedom, A major → D major.

---

## Lyrics

**VERSE 1**
No condemnation, none remains
For all who bear the Savior's name.
The Spirit of His life set free
What law and flesh could never be.

**VERSE 2**
The Father gave the only Son,
The Son obeyed till it was done.
The Spirit raised Him to His throne —
And claims a people as His own.

**PRE-CHORUS**
Father over earth and sea,
Son who came to set us free,
Spirit dwelling deep within —
He is for us, He has won.

**CHORUS**
If God is for us, who can stand against?
If God is for us, what could be the end?
Through every fire and through every flood,
Nothing can separate us from the love of God.
Nothing can separate us from the love of God.

**VERSE 3**
The whole creation groans and waits
For glory dawning past the gates.
He works in all things for our good —
The God who chose, redeemed, and stood.

**VERSE 4**
Christ at His right hand intercedes,
The Spirit prays our wordless needs.
The One who called us by His name
Forever will remain the same.

**PRE-CHORUS** *(repeat)*

**CHORUS** *(repeat)*

**BRIDGE** *(build across four repeats, key lift to E major on the third pass)*
Neither death nor life can divide,
Neither things to come nor things behind,
Neither height nor depth nor any power
Could take us from His side.
We are held in the love of God in Christ —
The love of God in Christ.

**FINAL CHORUS** *(full band, congregation)*
If God is for us, who can stand against?
If God is for us, what could be the end?
Through every fire and through every flood,
Nothing can separate us from the love of God.
Nothing can separate us from the love of God.

**OUTRO** *(soft, congregational, building one last time)*
Abba, Father — He is for us.
Abba, Father — He is for us.
Abba, Father — He is for us.

## Chord Sketch (Key of D)

- **Verses:** D · Bm · G · A
- **Pre-Chorus:** G · A · Bm · D
- **Chorus:** D · A · Bm · G (with A/C# pickup into the second line)
- **Bridge:** G · D · Bm · A (lift the third pass to E major for emotional crest)

Tempo 76–80 BPM. Begin sparse (acoustic guitar + piano pad). Verse 2 add bass + light kick. Pre-Chorus pull back, then the Chorus is full band. Bridge climbs four passes — the third lifts a whole step into E major, the fourth lands back in D for the Final Chorus. The Outro drops volume but keeps tempo, repeating "Abba, Father — He is for us" three times into a soft fade or a congregational hold on the IV chord.

## Connection to Romans 8

Verse 1 → **Romans 8:1-3** (no condemnation; the Spirit set free what the law could not)
Verse 2 → **Romans 8:3, 8:11, 8:15** (Father sent the Son; Spirit raised Christ; we are claimed as sons)
Pre-Chorus → **Romans 8:11, 8:15-16** (Trinitarian indwelling; the Spirit testifies; Abba Father)
Chorus → **Romans 8:31, 8:35-37, 8:38-39** ("If God is for us..."; more than conquerors; nothing separates)
Verse 3 → **Romans 8:18-25, 8:28-30** (creation groans; God works all things; called/justified/glorified)
Verse 4 → **Romans 8:26-27, 8:34** (Spirit intercedes; Christ at the right hand intercedes)
Bridge → **Romans 8:38-39** (sung nearly verbatim — death/life/things to come/height/depth)
Outro → **Romans 8:15** ("you received the Spirit of adoption by whom we cry out, 'Abba, Father'")

The chorus answers the Romans 7 cry "*who shall deliver me?*" with the chapter-8 declaration "*if God is for us, who can be against us?*" Where the Romans 7 song answers Paul's wretchedness with the bridegroom of Christ, this song answers it with the love of God that nothing can sever.

## Pairing with "Through Jesus Christ Our Lord"

| | Romans 7 | Romans 8 |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Through Jesus Christ Our Lord | If God Is For Us |
| Key | A major | D major *(IV — lifts the worshiper one step up)* |
| Tempo | 74 BPM | 78 BPM |
| Center | The Bridegroom who delivered us | The God who is for us, whose love nothing severs |
| Mood | Reverent, triumphant | Reverent, soaring |
| Function in service | Cry / answer | Declaration / sending |

Run them as a 2-song set: open with **Through Jesus Christ Our Lord** (the answer to Paul's cry), close with **If God Is For Us** (the declaration of who He is and what He has secured). The key change A → D and the tempo lift give a natural service arc.
